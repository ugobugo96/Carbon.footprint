This is a glossary of words related to a carbon footprint

Carbon Footprint

The total amount of greenhouse gases caused directly or indirectly by a person, event .

Fluorinated Gases

Fluorinated gases are human made chemicals that contain fluorine that traps heat inside the atmosphere

Greenhouse Gases

Greenhouse gases is the comabantion of all gasses that get trapped in the atmosphere and cause global warming.
