This is a glossary of words related to a carbon footprint

Carbon Footprint

The total amount of greenhouse gases caused directly or indirectly by a person or event.

Fluorinated Gases

Fluorinated gases are a group of synthetic greenhouse gases that contain fluorine.
They are used in various industrial applications, such as refrigeration, air conditioning, and insulation.
Fluorinated gases have a high global warming potential (GWP) and can contribute significantly to climate change when released into the atmosphere.

Greenhouse Gases

Greenhouse gases are the combination of specific gases that get trapped in the atmosphere and cause global warming, by reflecting heat back to the earth.

Unsustainable

Unsustainable refers to practices or behaviors that cannot be maintained over the long term without depleting limited resources or causing harm to the environment.

Sustainable

Sustainable refers to practices or behaviors that can be maintained over the long term without depleting limited resources or causing harm to the environment. Sustainable practices aim to meet the needs of the present without compromising the ability of future generations to meet their own needs. This can include using renewable resources, reducing waste, and minimizing environmental impact.
