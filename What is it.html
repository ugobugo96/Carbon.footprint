You may be wondering what a carbon footprint is.

A carbon footprint is an estimate of all greenhouse gases in our atmosphere, including methane,
nitrous oxide, and fluorinated gases .

Let's go into a more detailed explanation!

You may also have heard of a carbon footprint being called larger or smaller.
This is because different activities can release different amounts of greenhouse gases.

First, energy consumption can have a significant impact on your carbon footprint.
This is because a lot of energy is produced by fossil fuels, e.g. coal or oil.
Burning these fuels releases carbon dioxide into the atmosphere.
These fuels are classified as non-renewable because they take millions of years
to form, and they are being used up much faster than they can be replenished.
