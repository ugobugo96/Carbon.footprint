Here are some ways to reduce your carbon footprint:

1. Use energy-efficient appliances and light bulbs.

For example you may see a star rating on your washing machine you would want this
to be the maximum amount of stars because that means that there is less energy being used.

2. Reduce, reuse, and recycle to minimize waste.

You can use public transport or carpool to reduce the amount of cars on the road.
